Where’s the Catch

If it doesn’t cost anything, how does it work?

Two questions every broker asks in the first minute. Straight answers, so you can give them to your client without checking with us first.

Who pays Utopia

Utopia is a contracted in-network provider and bills the plan for the preventive nutrition counseling benefit — and only when someone actually completes an appointment. On fully insured plans that cost already sits inside the premium. On self-funded plans it arrives like any other claim, against a benefit the plan already covers and may already be paying out on.

That is the part worth repeating to a client: Utopia only gets paid if engagement actually happens, so the incentives line up with yours from day one.

Is there broker compensation included?

No — and that is deliberate. Utopia is a directly contracted provider practice, and sharing claim revenue is regulated. With no PEPM and no employer fees, there is nothing to build compensation out of in the first place.

So there is no commission, no revenue share, and nothing for you to disclose. What you get instead is the credit for putting a real clinical benefit in front of a client who will thank you for it.
