What Is Utopia

A benefit your clients already pay for, sitting there unused.

Utopia connects employees one-on-one with registered dietitians through telehealth. Every employee gets a customized health and nutrition program, built around their specific goals using a functional, root-cause approach. It's covered as a preventative benefit under the health plan, so your clients simply activate something that's already part of their coverage.
